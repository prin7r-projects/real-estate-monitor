# 06 — Sales Channels

The channel mix is **content + community + referral**. Paid acquisition is on the table only after
the organic mix proves a healthy CAC payback < 4 months.

## Channel 1 — Niche subreddit + Reddit AMAs (relocator persona)

- **Subreddits:** `r/IWantOut`, `r/AmerExit`, `r/Lisbon`, `r/Berlin`, `r/Madrid`, `r/AskNYC`,
  `r/Austin`, `r/RealEstate`, `r/RealEstateInvesting` (read-only for the last two, comment-only
  where helpful).
- **Pattern.** Drop a useful answer in a thread that isn't ours; only mention Skyline if a directly
  relevant question is asked. Quarterly AMA in the bigger relocator subs (`r/IWantOut`,
  `r/AmerExit`).
- **Why it fits.** The relocator persona reads these subs daily; recommendation friction is low when
  the post is a useful contribution first. Skyline can present screenshots of the alert flow that
  read as documentation, not promo.

## Channel 2 — Twitter / X + relocator-cred lists

- **Pattern.** Build a single voice account (`@SkylineWatch`) that posts the most striking weekly
  alerts (anonymized), short technical threads on the comp-baseline math, and occasional
  city-specific market temperature reports.
- **Reach amplifiers.** Real-estate-twitter has small but engaged operator clusters
  (RTR / multifamily / hard-money lenders). Cross-pollinate with `@SemiAnalysis`-style writers in
  the relocation-economy thesis.
- **Why it fits.** B2B operator persona converts on credibility signal more than on ad copy. The
  Twitter feed is the credibility surface.

## Channel 3 — Telegram channels (rent-side primary)

- **Pattern.** Open a public Telegram channel `@SkylineWatchPublic` that posts the day's top-5
  scored alerts (anonymized; city, score, residual, mode). Becomes a free preview of what paid
  subscribers receive.
- **Why it fits.** Rent-side relocators live on Telegram, particularly in EU cities. A channel of
  500–2,000 followers becomes a steady drip of "wait, that's a real listing under-market by 9% — I
  should subscribe."

## Channel 4 — RTR operator referral network

- **Pattern.** Investor-desk subscribers get a 1-month referral credit when a referred RTR operator
  signs up at the same tier. Single-page referral tracker in the dashboard (Wave 3).
- **Why it fits.** RTR operators talk to each other in small Telegram and Slack groups. One Marco
  who signs up brings 0.4 referred operators on average within 3 months (anchored against similar
  B2B SaaS data points).

## Channel 5 — Direct outbound to small brokerages

- **Pattern.** Sales Navigator + cold email to 50–80-person brokerage offices. Pitch isn't "replace
  your MLS" but "give your buyer-side agents a queue for their clients' must-haves." Investor desk
  multi-seat fits.
- **Why it fits.** Small brokerage offices ($10M–$50M GCI) lack tooling for buyer-side rep. Skyline's
  API plus multi-seat is a natural sub-license inside a brokerage.
- **Caveat.** This channel is carefully gated — we are not an MLS replacement, and we will refund
  any brokerage that signs up expecting to be one.

## Channel 6 — Newsletter cross-posts (sale-side primary)

- **Pattern.** Cross-post weekly market-temperature summaries to existing finance / real-estate
  newsletters with audience overlap (e.g. independent newsletters in the Bigger Pockets / RTR
  Operator / mid-tier real-estate-twitter cohort). Trade traffic for citation.
- **Why it fits.** Newsletter readers are typically operators 2–5 years into a portfolio; they're
  the secondary persona. Cross-post bylines build SEO authority slower but more durably.

## Channel 7 — Conference + meetup (long-tail)

- **Pattern.** Sponsor / speak at small operator-only events (BiggerPockets-Con, Meetup-tier
  multifamily / RTR events, EU fintech / proptech meetups in Berlin / Lisbon).
- **Cadence.** 1–2 events per quarter, founder-only attendance. No sales reps.
- **Why it fits.** Investor-desk persona converts on in-person credibility. Conferences are slow
  but durable; one Marco closed at a meetup is 18–36 months of revenue.

## Channels we explicitly avoid

- **Google Search Ads on "rent in <city>" keywords.** Loses to Zillow / Realtor.com on bid; CAC
  unsustainable.
- **Facebook / Instagram lookalike audiences.** Wrong persona match. We are not a homebuying coach.
- **TikTok influencer placement.** The persona doesn't make decisions on TikTok content.

## First 90-day channel commitment

Concentrate on channels 1, 3, and 4 — the cheapest to test and the closest match to the relocator
persona. Twitter (channel 2) takes a back seat; we post but don't push. Channel 5 (brokerage
outbound) starts in month 4 once the dashboard ships.
